# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite + Bun + TypeScript, front-end only, static deploy on Render (Expressers house stack, same as alberto-charamba).

## Users

Decision-makers at Angolan companies and institutions (C-suite, sustainability/HSE leads, banks, public bodies) who need to meet ESG and sustainability expectations, plus partners and sponsors of the Fórum Nacional de Negócios Sustentáveis.

## Product Purpose

Institutional website for ALLORA (ALLORA | ESG, Allora SU LDA), an Angolan consulting agency in ESG and sustainability: "Soluções para empresas e instituições". Tagline: "Cuidamos do Amanhã Hoje!". It must present the consultancy (proposal requested by Alberto Charamba, reference erm.com, with less content than ERM) and feature its flagship initiative, the Fórum Nacional de Negócios Sustentáveis (FNNS).

## Positioning

Angolan-rooted ESG practice that measures and acts: carbon footprint measurement and offsetting (Selo Verde de Compensação de Carbono, carbon-neutral stands e.g. BFA at FILDA), field work, institutional programs, and convening the public and private sectors through the FNNS (B2B/B2G meetings, +500 participants, Hotel Intercontinental, 2nd edition 10 March 2026).
