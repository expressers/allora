# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite + Bun + TypeScript, front-end only, multi-page with build-time pre-rendering, static deploy on Vercel.

## Users

Decision-makers at Angolan companies and institutions (C-suite, sustainability/HSE leads, banks, public bodies) who need to meet ESG and sustainability expectations, plus partners and sponsors of the Fórum Nacional de Negócios Sustentáveis.

## Product Purpose

Institutional website for ALLORA (ALLORA | ESG, Allora SU LDA), an Angolan consulting agency in ESG and sustainability: "Soluções para empresas e instituições". Tagline: "Cuidamos do Amanhã Hoje!". It presents the company (Sobre), its five services, the Carbonómetro, its projects and initiatives, the Fórum Nacional de Negócios Sustentáveis (FNNS), the Academia ALLORA and its contacts. Reference: erm.com, with less content than ERM.

## Positioning

Angolan-rooted ESG practice that measures and acts: carbon footprint measurement and offsetting (Carbonómetro, carbon-neutral stands such as BFA, BCS and OPAIA at FILDA 2026), environmental licensing (EIA, PGA, PGR; 30+ companies licensed), training (Academia, 800+ professionals), social impact (Papel Semente, EcoBag, Hortas Comunitárias, 1.000+ community washbasins), and convening the public and private sectors through the FNNS (two editions, +500 participants, 2nd edition 10 March 2026 at Hotel InterContinental, 47 t CO₂ offset).

## Open decisions

- Email domain (allora.ao vs allora.co.ao vs alloraesg.com), Facebook and YouTube URLs.
- What the Nigéria / Brasil / África do Sul map in the PDF means (the site says "com parceiros em").
- The "o que inclui" lists on the services page are derived from the PDF's descriptions and need the client's validation.
- The PDF's partner slide also shows "We support UN Global Compact" and "Empresa B Certificada"; left out of the partner wall until the client confirms what they mean.
- Real photography beyond Instagram, forum and PDF material to be supplied by the client (the PDF crops are low resolution).
- The contact form opens the visitor's email or WhatsApp with the message filled in; a real form service (Formspree, Web3Forms) needs an account under the ALLORA email.
